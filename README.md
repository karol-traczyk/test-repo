## Gift Ideas

Looking for the perfect gift? Whether you're shopping on a budget or ready to splurge, this curated list has something for everyone. From practical everyday items to memorable experiences, these ideas are designed to inspire and delight.

### Budget-Friendly ($10–$30)

- **Insulated Travel Mug** (~$15) – Keep drinks hot or cold for hours, perfect for commuters
- **Desk Plant Kit** (~$18) – Small succulents or herb garden for home or office
- **Quality Notebook & Pen Set** (~$20) – Premium writing supplies for journaling or note-taking
- **Cozy Throw Blanket** (~$25) – Soft fleece or knit blanket for movie nights
- **Board Game or Puzzle** (~$20–$30) – Classic entertainment for game nights with friends

### Mid-Range ($30–$100)

- **Wireless Earbuds** (~$50) – Convenient audio for music, podcasts, and calls
- **Cooking Class Voucher** (~$60) – Learn a new cuisine or technique in a hands-on session
- **Portable Bluetooth Speaker** (~$45) – Take the music anywhere, indoors or outdoors
- **Quality Chef's Knife** (~$70) – Essential kitchen tool that makes cooking more enjoyable
- **Subscription Box (3-month)** (~$75) – Coffee, books, snacks, or hobby supplies delivered monthly

### Premium ($100+)

- **Smartwatch or Fitness Tracker** (~$150–$300) – Track health goals and stay connected
- **Weekend Getaway Experience** (~$200–$500) – Bed & breakfast stay or local adventure package
- **High-Quality Backpack** (~$120) – Durable travel or everyday carry with thoughtful design
- **Professional Massage or Spa Package** (~$150) – Relaxation and self-care experience
- **Premium Headphones** (~$200–$350) – Noise-canceling audio for immersive listening
