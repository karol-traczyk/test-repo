## A Poem

Lines of code, like thoughts take form,
Each function built to weather storm.
Bugs may hide in logic's seam,
But tests will guard the developer's dream.

Commit by commit, the work takes shape,
Through trials, errors, no escape.
Yet in the merge, the branches meet,
And something whole becomes complete.

Build with care, refactor true,
For those who follow after you.
In every loop and every call,
We craft the tools that serve us all.
